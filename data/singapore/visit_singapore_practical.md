# Visit Singapore: Practical Travel Guidelines

## Climate & Weather
Singapore has an equatorial tropical rainforest climate (Köppen Af) with no distinct seasons:
- Daily temperatures stay between 27°C and 32°C.
- Relative humidity remains between 70% and 90%.
- Afternoon thunderstorms are frequent and sudden throughout the year.

## Currency & Cashless Payments
- The legal tender is the Singapore Dollar (SGD).
- Cashless transactions (credit cards, NETS, contactless digital wallets) are accepted across almost all transit and commercial venues. Minor cash is useful only for traditional hawker stalls.

## Accessibility & Traveling with Children
- Stroller and Wheelchair Infrastructure: All MRT stations have elevator access, wide access gates, and barrier-free pathways.
- Low-floor public buses offer accessible boarding ramps.
- Major museum complexes, shopping malls, and conservatories are fully air-conditioned and equipped with ramps and nursing rooms.
