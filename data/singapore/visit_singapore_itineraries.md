# Visit Singapore: Sample Itineraries & Activity Classifications

## Indoor Attractions (Rain-Safe)
- Cloud Forest & Flower Dome: Large climate-controlled glass conservatories at Gardens by the Bay.
- Jewel Changi Airport: Features the 40-meter indoor HSBC Rain Vortex waterfall and Canopy Park.
- ArtScience Museum: Interactive digital art exhibits located along Marina Bay.
- National Gallery Singapore: Houses Southeast Asian art inside the restored Supreme Court and City Hall.
- National Museum of Singapore: Interactive historical and cultural galleries along Stamford Road.
- S.E.A. Aquarium: Large indoor marine park located on Sentosa Island.

## Outdoor Attractions (Requires Clear Weather)
- Gardens by the Bay Outdoor Grounds: Free-access Supertree Grove, Dragonfly Lake, and Heritage Gardens.
- Singapore Botanic Gardens: UNESCO World Heritage site featuring the National Orchid Garden.
- Mandai Wildlife Reserve: Open-air wildlife settings including Singapore Zoo, Night Safari, and River Wonders.
- Sentosa Beaches: Outdoor recreation across Siloso, Palawan, and Tanjong beaches.
- Southern Ridges: 10-kilometer outdoor elevated nature trail connecting Mount Faber Park and Henderson Waves.

## Curated 3-Day Blueprint
- Day 1 (Civic & Marina Bay): Merlion Park, National Gallery, Marina Bay waterfront, and Spectra Light & Water Show.
- Day 2 (Cultural Enclaves): Walking excursions through Chinatown, Little India, and Kampong Glam.
- Day 3 (Nature & Entertainment): Mandai Wildlife Reserve or Sentosa Island attractions.
