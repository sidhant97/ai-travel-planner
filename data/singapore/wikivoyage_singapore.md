# Singapore Travel Guide - Wikivoyage

## Districts & Highlights
Singapore is a global city-state in Southeast Asia.
- Marina Bay: The modern business and entertainment epicenter. Highlights include Marina Bay Sands, Merlion Park, and the Singapore Flyer.
- Chinatown: Preserved heritage shophouses, open-air markets, and the Buddha Tooth Relic Temple.
- Little India: Vibrant commercial enclave along Serangoon Road featuring the Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple and Mustafa Centre.
- Kampong Glam: Historic Malay-Arab district known for the golden-domed Sultan Mosque and boutique shopping on Haji Lane.
- Sentosa Island: Coastal resort island with white-sand beaches (Siloso, Palawan), theme parks, and the S.E.A. Aquarium.

## Public Transportation Network
- Mass Rapid Transit (MRT): Highly efficient, clean, and extensive. Foreign contactless Visa/Mastercard credit cards and EZ-Link cards work directly at all turnstiles.
- Public Buses: Island-wide coverage with distance-based fares. Tap your card when boarding and exiting.
- Taxis & Ride-Hailing: Grab, Gojek, and standard metered taxis operate universally. Surcharges apply during peak hours and midnight to 6:00 AM.

## Dining & Hawker Culture
Hawker centres provide hygienic, authentic local street cuisine. Key locations include Maxwell Food Centre, Chinatown Complex, Lau Pa Sat, and Old Airport Road. Iconic dishes include Hainanese Chicken Rice, Katong Laksa, and Chili Crab. Tap water across the island is completely safe to drink.

## Local Laws & Rules
- Sale and import of chewing gum is prohibited.
- Littering, smoking outside designated yellow boxes, and jaywalking result in spot fines starting at 300 SGD.
- Eating or drinking inside MRT stations or trains is strictly prohibited, carrying fines up to 500 SGD.
